BU Series

Bypass Circuit Unit · Precision & E/P Regulator Options

The Bypass Circuit Unit reduces pressurization time for parts with large volume, helping cut total test time. Air Leak Testers control the Air Operated Valve(s) of the Bypass Circuit Unit — select Single, Dual or Triple valves depending on the shape and volume of the tested parts.

E/P Regulator Bypass Circuit Units are also available for Air Leak Testers.

Tester-Controlled Operation

The Air Leak Tester controls the operation of the Bypass Circuit Unit directly.

🔌

Selectable Circuit

Circuit is selectable among Single, Dual and Triple depending on the shape and volume of the part to be tested.

⚙️

E/P Regulator Option

An Electro-Pneumatic (E/P) regulator is also available, applicable only for Air Leak Testers.
