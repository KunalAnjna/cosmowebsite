Application Overview

The DF-R280 is a digital flowmeter built around interchangeable Laminar Flow Tubes, measuring air flow with high precision across a range of 10 mL/min to 500 L/min. Up to 8 Laminar Flow Tubes can be connected to a single unit for multi-range coverage.

A color touchscreen with Advanced, Waveform and Simple measure screens, built-in temperature/line-pressure/atmospheric-pressure sensors, and automatic compensation keep readings stable and comparable across changing line conditions.

With 100-channel storage, dual RS-232C ports, a standard USB port, and an optional FTP-ready 16G internal memory, the DF-R280 suits both in-line production testing and laboratory examination.
