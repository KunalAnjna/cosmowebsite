Application Overview

The LS-1866 is built for production lines where floor space and budget are both tight, but detection ability still needs to be excellent. Its heat-resistant, pressure-resistant construction and intelligent air circuit make it well suited to demanding, high-cycle testing environments.

The tester supports 16 independent test channels, making it practical for lines that handle multiple workpiece variants without needing a separate controller per part family. Master preset and NR (noise reduction) mode work together to keep detection accurate even when ambient or process noise is unavoidable.

A tool-free quick-mounting bracket lets the whole unit be removed and reinstalled quickly, which suits flexible lines with frequent changeover, while worldwide power-cable options (VA/VE/VK) simplify deployment in different markets.
