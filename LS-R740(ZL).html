Application Overview

The LS-R740(ZL) is a differential pressure air leak tester purpose-built for small sealed parts. The part to be tested is placed in a capsule and pressurized to measure the pressure change caused by leak flowing into the part.

Its controller and pneumatic circuit unit are built as separate modules, with the sensor placed close to the target capsule for improved response and measurement stability. Easy volume compensation, when the tested part and reference part differ in volume, keeps setup simple across product variants.

With 32-channel program storage, multi-language support (English, Japanese, Chinese), and USB/RS-232C data management, the LS-R740(ZL) suits water-proof components and finished products requiring an IP Protection Rating check.
