Application Overview

The LZ-1550 is a semi-automatic, micro-volume detection type air leak tester system built into a desktop machine. This tester is ideal for water-proof components or finished products which require an IP Protection Rating.

Thanks to the built-in clamp mechanism, the operator simply sets the workpiece — it is automatically clamp-sealed and the leak test is carried out. Being a desktop machine, it suits a variety of workspaces, and has been made smaller and lighter than conventional models in pursuit of user-friendliness.

It is suitable for a wide range of devices, from relatively small electrical components such as relays, crystal oscillators, and image sensors, to larger workpieces such as mobile phones, transmitters, and rectifiers.
