Application Overview

The DF-231BA is a mass-flow gauge built for fast, stable in-line measurement. Because its Mass Flow sensor responds directly to mass flow, no temperature or pressure correction is required — simplifying setup and reducing drift over time.

A response time of approximately 300 ms (99% step response) makes the DF-231BA well suited to rapid cycle testing, while its 10-channel memory and 4-level (HH/HI/LO/LL) limit settings support flexible production-line configurations.

With RS-232C communication, BCD output, and a compact panel-mount form factor, the DF-231BA integrates easily into existing test benches and control panels.
