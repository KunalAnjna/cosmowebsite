Application Overview

The AF-R221 is a sophisticated air flow tester optimized for testing discharge flow and airtightness. Its flow sensor is selectable from Laminar Flow or Mass Flow, allowing the same platform to cover a wide variety of production-line and laboratory needs.

An icon-driven touchscreen interface, chart-based monitoring of pressure and flow, and standard Flow Check & Flow Optimizer functions keep testing accurate even as line pressure fluctuates.

With 32-channel program storage, four-language support, USB and dual RS-232C data management, and an optional FTP function, the AF-R221 fits into automated, laborsaving production environments.
