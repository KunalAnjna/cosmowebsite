Application Overview

The LS-R740(SS) is purpose-built for micro-volume parts where even a very small leak represents a meaningful proportion of internal volume. The part is set on a dedicated fixture, pressurized internally, and the resulting pressure change generated by a leak is measured with the Y-circuit's minimum-internal-volume pneumatic design.

Optional Auto Leak Calibration (K01/K001) and a calibration port with Leak Master compatibility (JX) let the system be verified against a known reference leak, which matters most at micro-volume scale where sensor and fixture drift would otherwise dominate the reading.

Panel-mount (P) and standard bench configurations, together with selectable sensor sensitivity (KX1/KX05) and cable-length options, make the LS-R740(SS) adaptable to compact fixture cells as well as integrated production stations.
