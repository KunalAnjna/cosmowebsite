LM-1C / LM-1AH

Leak Master · for High Pressure up to 4.9 MPa

The Leak Master LM-1C is ideal for everyday inspections of air leak testers. LM-1AH is for high pressure applications up to 4.9 MPa. Manufactured to your specified test pressure and leak volume.

↓ Download Catalogue Explore Features
Edge & Function

A reliable, repeatable
reference leak for every check

Three models are available: LM-1C (customized to specified pressure & flow), LM-1C-J1 (standard model with predetermined flow), and LM-1AH for high pressure from 1 MPa to 4.9 MPa.

🎯

Customized Pressure & Flow

Manufactured to the specified pressure and flow, with accuracy (difference from required flow) within ±5%.

🔬

High Precision at Low Flow

For 2 mL/min or less: within ±10 to 20%. High-pressure LM: 0.1–0.7 mL/min within ±20%, 0.8–50 mL/min within ±10%.

✅

Reliable Sensitivity Checks

Enables easy and reliable verification of Air Leak Tester sensitivity.

⏱️

Long-Term Stability

Maintains the same flow for a long period of time.

🔄

Secondary Pressure Method

Can also be used for the Secondary Pressure method.

🔌

Direct CAL Port Connection

Can be connected to the CAL port of Air Leak Testers, or between work and tester piping using the dedicated adapter.

🔁

Quick Return to Test State

Replacing the Leak Master with the dedicated plug lets the tester easily return to normal (no-leak) test state.

📜

Traceability Certificate

A traceability certificate is available upon request.
