Application Overview

The LS-R912 extends the LS-R902's touch-panel ease of use with two independent test-pressure circuits, Inlet A and Inlet B. This suits parts that need two different pressure ranges tested at a single location, or two separate test locations on the same part that each require a different pressure — with the caveat that Inlet A and Inlet B cannot run simultaneously.

A large numeric keypad appears during setup so any parameter can be adjusted through the same simple screen flow used across the LS-R900 family, and pressure can be fine-tuned either on the workpiece itself or off it, depending on what the process calls for.

32-channel storage (extendable to 100 with RX11), dual RS-232C ports, USB, and an FTP-ready LAN port give the LS-R912 the same factory-integration options as the LS-R902, while covering a wider span of pressure ranges — from precision micro-pressure up to 4.9 MPa extremely-high-pressure testing.
