Application Overview

The LS-R700 is designed for efficient quality control in production lines, offering a compact body with all the core functions needed for differential pressure leak testing. Its vertical form factor and guidance bar make it particularly suited to lines where operators rotate frequently or setup time needs to be minimised.

The system handles a wide range of part volumes and pressure requirements — from micro-pressure (5 kPa) through to extremely high pressure (4.9 MPa) — making it versatile across automotive, electronics, and general manufacturing applications.

With fieldbus options (DeviceNet, CC-Link, Profibus, EtherNet/IP, PROFINET), test result data is pushed directly to factory PLCs, enabling real-time quality monitoring. The optional 100-channel program storage (RX11) suits high-mix production without the need for a separate controller.
